How this report is built

Each recording is a stream of ~1 s chunks with an A-weighted level (dBA) and optional YAMNet / gate metadata.

Time budget charts classify every chunk: gated chunks count as Relative silence; otherwise the chunk’s eligible YAMNet label is mapped to a macro category (Traffic & Transit, Human & Community, and so on). Shares are duration-weighted (chunk count × ~1 s), including silence. Day is 07:00–21:59 local time; night is 22:00–06:59.

Acoustic events (used for the takeaway’s “driven primarily by…” line) are separate: continuous runs of chunks at or above the active dBA threshold, with up to max gap quieter chunks bridged inside one event. Events shorter than the minimum length are dropped. The active threshold is either a fixed dBA value or selection L90 plus an offset — set on the Data tab before you generate the report.

Noise-profile hourly levels use energetic Leq; soft bands show within-hour L90–L10; bars show the share of noisy moments (ungated chunks — when the gate was open) in that hour.

Acoustic Comfort Rating (A–E) starts at 100 points and subtracts three capped penalties, then maps the result to a letter grade with a neutral label (Tranquil → Intense):

1) Nighttime sound floor (max −40): energetic Leq of night chunks (22:00–06:59). Subtract 2 points for every dBA above 40. Example: 48 dBA → −16.

2) Daytime baseline (max −35): median (L50) of day chunks (07:00–21:59). Subtract 1.5 points for every dBA above 45. Example: 54 dBA → −13.5.

3) Volatility (max −25): share of noisy (ungated) moments across the whole selection. Subtract 1 point for every percentage point above 10%. Example: 22% noisy → −12.

Grades: A 85–100 Tranquil · B 70–84 Balanced · C 55–69 Active · D 40–54 Vibrant · E below 40 Intense.

If the selection has only day or only night data, that period’s penalty is omitted and the remaining penalties are rescaled so they still span a 100-point score. A short warning notes that coverage was incomplete.

L90 dynamic gate: open = ambient floor (10th percentile over ~5 min) + this offset (default 5 dB). Gate closes after 2 consecutive chunks below open or when the signal is too cold. AGC tracks the L90 floor. Peak 200 ms sub-windows and a 4 dB ΔRMS jump can also open the gate for short impulses.
